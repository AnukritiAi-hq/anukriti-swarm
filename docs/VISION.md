# Vision

> Where we're going — and why it matters.

---

## The Opportunity

The human genome has been sequenced for over two decades. Pharmacogenomic guidelines exist. Population-specific allele frequency data is publicly available. Yet **billions of people** are still prescribed drugs based on population averages rather than their genetic reality.

This isn't a technology gap. It's an **architecture gap**.

No system exists that can reason about genomics the way a team of specialists would — combining population context, gene-level expertise, published evidence, and safety verification into a single, auditable decision.

**Anukriti Swarm is that system.**

---

## The 5-Phase Journey

### Phase 1 — Population-Aware Pharmacogenomic Simulation
*Where we are today.*

A working proof-of-concept demonstrating that population context fundamentally changes pharmacogenomic interpretation. Nine specialist agents collaborate to analyze drug-gene-population interactions with full evidence grounding and verification.

**Milestone:** "14% of South Asians can't activate clopidogrel — and our system catches it."

**Delivered:**
- 3 genes, 3 populations, 3 drugs
- Deterministic reasoning (no hallucinations)
- Evidence-grounded outputs (100% cited)
- 6-check verification with TAO escalation
- <2ms end-to-end execution

---

### Phase 2 — Distributed Genomic Intelligence Agents
*Next 6 months.*

Scale from simulation to real-world integration. Connect to live pharmacogenomic databases via MCP. Process real VCF files. Support all CPIC-actionable gene-drug pairs (~40 genes, ~100 drugs).

**Milestone:** "Any VCF file in, evidence-backed pharmacogenomic report out."

**Key deliverables:**
- MCP integration (MongoDB, Qdrant, gnomAD)
- Real VCF parsing and variant calling
- Full CPIC gene coverage
- API service for clinical research platforms
- Multi-sample batch processing
- Real embedding models for retrieval

---

### Phase 3 — Chromosome & Pathway Specialist Swarms
*12–18 months.*

Expand from individual gene reasoning to systems-level genomic intelligence. Chromosome agents analyze structural variants. Pathway agents reason about multi-gene interactions. The swarm becomes a **genome-scale reasoning engine**.

**Milestone:** "Whole-genome pharmacogenomic analysis in under 60 seconds."

**Key deliverables:**
- 25 parallel chromosome agents
- Structural variant detection (CNV, deletions, duplications)
- Multi-gene pathway reasoning (drug metabolism cascades)
- Polygenic risk integration
- Drug-drug-gene interaction modeling
- Real-time guideline update ingestion

---

### Phase 4 — Federated Genomic Intelligence Infrastructure
*18–36 months.*

Enable cross-institutional genomic reasoning without sharing raw patient data. Hospitals, biobanks, and research institutions contribute to a shared intelligence layer while maintaining data sovereignty.

**Milestone:** "Federated pharmacogenomic intelligence across 10 institutions."

**Key deliverables:**
- Federated learning for population frequency refinement
- Cross-institutional variant interpretation consensus
- Privacy-preserving multi-site analysis
- Regulatory compliance framework (HIPAA, GDPR, GINA)
- Institutional deployment packages
- Population-specific model calibration from local data

---

### Phase 5 — Autonomous Biomedical Reasoning Systems
*36+ months.*

The long-term vision: a general-purpose biomedical reasoning infrastructure that extends beyond pharmacogenomics to any domain where genetic variation meets clinical decision-making. Oncology. Rare disease. Prenatal screening. Preventive medicine.

**Milestone:** "Autonomous biomedical reasoning that knows what it knows, knows what it doesn't, and asks for help when it should."

**Key deliverables:**
- Multi-omics integration (genomics + transcriptomics + proteomics)
- Longitudinal patient reasoning (temporal genomic context)
- Autonomous literature monitoring and guideline integration
- Self-improving verification (learn from escalation outcomes)
- Clinical decision support certification pathway
- Global health equity deployment (low-resource settings)

## Why This Wins

### The Moat

1. **Architecture, not model.** We're not fine-tuning an LLM. We're building the orchestration layer that makes genomic AI trustworthy. Models change. Architecture endures.

2. **Population-first design.** Every competitor treats population as an afterthought. We treat it as the primary reasoning dimension. This is structurally impossible to bolt on later.

3. **Verification as infrastructure.** Our safety layer isn't a filter — it's the architecture. Every output is verified before delivery. This is the standard healthcare AI will require.

4. **Federated-ready from day one.** The multi-agent architecture naturally distributes across institutions. Monolithic systems cannot federate.

## Principles That Don't Change

No matter how far we scale:

1. **Deterministic science stays deterministic.** We will never use an LLM to assign a phenotype.
2. **Every claim is traceable.** If we can't cite it, we don't say it.
3. **Population context is required.** We will never deliver a population-blind result.
4. **Uncertainty is surfaced, not hidden.** The system always tells you what it doesn't know.
5. **Human oversight is preserved.** We augment experts. We don't replace them.
6. **Research-first, clinical-later.** We earn clinical trust through demonstrated safety, not shortcuts.
